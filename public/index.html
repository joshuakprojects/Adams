Required fields

Tick what must be filled in before a work order can be saved. Staff can still Save as draft (for jobs booked ahead); drafts can't be texted to drivers until they're finished. A pin dropped on the map counts as the address.

QuickBooks Online

Every work order with prices gets a pending Estimate in QuickBooks, updated as the job goes (prices, loads, the driver's report, done). The bookkeeper turns it into an invoice in QuickBooks; after that the app leaves it alone. Cancelled work orders mark their Estimate rejected.
