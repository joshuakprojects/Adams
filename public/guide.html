Start hereWho sees what

In shortOffice logins get the reception screen. Dispatchers get the Board and can text drivers. Admins also get Setup.

Everyone logs in at dispatch.joshtemp.site (later dispatch.adamsbros.ca). What you see depends on your login.

Office / receptionOffice login

The reception screen: take a call, price it, book it, find a customer, answer questions, and the Our sites page. Can make work orders but can't text drivers.

DispatcherDispatcher login

The Board: everything office can do, plus assign drivers, text them, mark jobs done, read drivers' texts and the Records tab.

Admin / ownerAdmin login

Everything, plus Setup: drivers, trucks, logins, prices, work order fields, site heads, equipment, texting times and backups.

The routineA day with the app

In shortCalls get booked all day. At 2:30 PM site heads are asked what they need. The office hands it out and confirms. At 6:30 AM drivers get their day. They reply OK, then DONE.

Times are the usual settings. An admin can change them under Setup.

  1. All dayReception

    Customer calls are priced and booked on the reception screen. Website orders land in Website requests on the Board, ready to schedule.

  2. 2:30 PMAutomatic

    Every active site head gets a text asking what they need for the next work day. On Friday it asks about Monday.

  3. AfternoonSite heads

    They reply in plain words ("tandem AM, mini ex, 2 labourers"), tap the link, or call in. Replies show on the Our sites page.

  4. Before closeOffice

    On Our sites, give each site its trucks and drivers, machines and labourers, then Save. Machines that need moving get their float work order made automatically. Press Send confirmations so each site head knows what's coming.

  5. 6:30 AMAutomatic

    Each driver and labourer gets one text with that day's jobs: where, what, their own load, and a link to the job page. Drivers are only told the morning of.

  6. During the jobDrivers

    They reply OK to confirm, tap +1 load on the job page, text a photo of the scale ticket, and reply DONE when finished. The Board updates as they go.

  7. After the jobAutomatic, if on

    An hour after every driver is done, the customer can get a text asking for a Google review (once a year at most, 9 AM to 8 PM).

DispatchersThe Board

In shortEach card is a work order. Cards move from To text to Waiting for OK to Working to Done as drivers reply. The yellow button is the next step.

One day at a time. Every work order is a card, sorted into lanes by where it's at.

Date bar
‹ Today › and the date picker move between days. Lanes / List / Map / Calendar change the view.
Tiles
Active, Needs attention (not texted, no reply, a problem), Done today, Drivers free, and Website requests waiting to be scheduled. Click one to filter.
Type chips
Show one kind of work order. 🏗️ Our site shows only work for our own job sites.
Drivers row
Each driver's jobs for the day and whether they've been texted.
Lanes
To text → Waiting for OK → Working → Done. A card moves along by itself as drivers reply.
Text drivers their day
Sends every driver their list for the day on screen now, if you don't want to wait for the morning text.

Reading a card

  • Top line: work order number, time (First thing / AM / PM / Any time) and type. Work for our own sites says OUR SITE, floats moving our machines too.
  • Middle: what it is, where from and to, customer or site contact.
  • Driver line: each driver, their truck, and their status: Not texted Texted – waiting Confirmed ✓ Working Done ✓. Needs update means the job changed after they were texted.
  • Big button: the one thing to do next, e.g. Text 1 driver, Re-send text, Mark complete.
  • More: print, copy to another day, history, book pick-up (bins and toilets), cancel or delete.

EveryoneMaking a work order

In short+ New job, pick the type, fill in when, who and where, tick the drivers, Save. Saving never texts anyone.

  1. Press + New job (or New call on reception) and pick the type: Aggregate, Bin, Septic, Toilet, Float, Labour, Our site or Misc. The form changes to match the paper form for that type.
  2. Fill in the date and time (First thing, AM, PM or Any time), the customer and the address. Start typing a name to pick a past customer.
  3. Prices fill in from the price book as you go. Drivers never see prices.
  4. Dispatchers tick the drivers and pick each one's truck.
  5. Save work order. If something required is missing, the form says what. Booking far ahead with details to come? Use Save as draft: drafts can't be texted until finished.
Saving never texts anyone. Drivers get it in the morning jobs text, or when a dispatcher presses the button on the card.

Office & dispatchOur sites

In shortRead what the site head asked for, give trucks, drivers, machines and labourers, Save, then Send confirmations. Floats for machines are made for you.

For our own jobs: the trucks, drivers, machines and labourers each site head needs. This replaces the end-of-day phone calls and the paper sheet. Open it from the Our sites tab on the Board, or 🏗️ Our sites on reception.

Working through a site card

  1. Read what they asked for. The green chip says how they replied (Replied by text, on the link, or no reply yet). Their words are shown as sent, and below it the app's reading, Asked for. Took it on the phone? Type it in the box and press Save.
  2. Check what's already there. On site now lists the machines sitting at that site, since when and where they came from. Done with one? Back to the yard makes the float work order to bring it home.
  3. Trucks & drivers. Rows start from what they asked for. Pick the truck and the driver: a driver's usual truck fills in by itself. For tandems and triaxles, fill in what it's hauling and how many loads. The lists say if a truck or driver is already out, e.g. "customer: Smith" or "at Carling".
  4. Machines. Add the machines they need. The list says where each one is. If a machine isn't at the site yet, saving makes its float work order automatically, from wherever the app has it. It shows on the Board as an OUR SITE float: give it a float truck and driver there.
  5. Labourers and a note, e.g. "float arrives 7:30". The note goes to the site head.
  6. Save. That makes or updates the day's Our site work order on the Board.
  7. When every site is done, press Send confirmations at the top. Each site head gets a text listing what they're getting. Drivers are not texted until the morning.

The yard strip

Other buttons. Text all site heads sends the "what do you need?" text now. Text Mike on a card re-asks just that site head. The ‹ › arrows look at other days, e.g. Friday planning for Monday.

AdminsSetup

In shortPeople, trucks, logins, text times, work order fields, sites, equipment and backups. Changes take effect right away.

Set once, change any time. Only admins see this tab.

  • Drivers, labourers & trucksNames, cell numbers and each driver's usual truck.
  • LoginsWho can log in, and as office, dispatcher or admin. The owner manages these.
  • Our sitesSite heads (name, cell), sites (name, address, who runs it, Active) and equipment (name, kind, unit #, where it is now, in the shop). Tick the box to text site heads automatically at 2:30 PM.
  • Drivers' jobs textSet to for today, the morning of, at 6:30 AM so drivers hear about their day that morning.
  • Work order fieldsRename, hide, reorder or add the boxes on each type of work order.
  • Required fieldsWhat must be filled in before a work order saves.
  • Review requestsPaste the Google review link and switch on the after-job review text.
  • BackupsCopies every hour and night. The owner can restore everything, or only Our sites.

EveryoneOther tabs

In shortCustomers, Prices, Questions and Records: look things up, keep the price book and answers current, and pull old work orders.

Customers
Everyone we've done work for, built up from work orders. Search by name, phone or address, open a customer to see their past work orders, and see who's due for a septic pump-out.
Prices
The price book the app prices work orders from: aggregates, delivery by distance, septic, bins, toilets and water. Admins change prices here; every change is logged, and work orders keep the price they were booked at.
Questions
Standard answers to what customers ask, so everyone gives the same answer. Reception sees them under Customer asks.
Records
Dispatchers: find work orders by date range, status, type or name; print a batch; or download a spreadsheet, e.g. for tax time.

When something looks offGood to know

In shortMost problems are a driver not texted yet, a machine recorded in the wrong place, or a site head who hasn't replied.

A driver says they didn't get their job.

Check their line on the card. Not texted means it hasn't gone yet: press the big button on the card. Needs update means the job changed: press Re-send text. Check their cell number under Setup.

A site head didn't reply.

Their card says no reply yet. Tap their number to call, or Text Mike to ask again. Type what they say into the box on the card.

How does the app know where a machine is?

From what's been done in the app: the last site it was given to, the last float that moved it, or the last time it was sent back to the yard. A new machine starts in the yard. When you start out, an admin sets each machine's real spot under Setup → Our sites → Equipment → Where it is now (no float is made).

A machine shows at the wrong site.

It was moved without the app. An admin fixes it under Setup → Our sites → Equipment → Where it is now: that changes it straight away and makes no float.

I gave a site a machine by mistake.

Remove it on the card and Save. Its float work order is removed too, as long as no driver has been texted about it.

A job finished early or was called off.

Dispatchers: More → Mark complete, or More → Cancel job. Drivers who were texted are told when a job is cancelled.

Where did a text from a driver go?

Texts the app understood (OK, DONE, photos) go on the job. Anything else is flagged under All texts from drivers on the Board.
